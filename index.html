Questions

Before you order.

Which sites do you support?

Chess.com and Lichess. We read the public game history for the username you give us, so there’s nothing to log in to.

Which rating goes on the poster?

The time control you’ve played most, like rapid or blitz. If you’d rather show a different one, say so when you reply to your proof.

How many games do I need?

Around 50 rated games makes a good poster. Thousands of games work too: for very active players, each dot on the Every game design stands for a small batch of games, and the poster says so.

Can I give one as a gift?

Yes. You only need their username, since game histories are public. Put your own email at checkout and we’ll send the proof to you.

Do you post outside the UK?

Prints go to UK addresses for now. The digital file works anywhere, and any print shop can print it.

What if something’s wrong with my print?

Every poster is made to order, so we check the proof with you first. If a print arrives damaged or doesn’t match the approved proof, we’ll reprint it free.
